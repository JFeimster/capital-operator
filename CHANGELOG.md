The format is based on [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.0.0/),
and this project adheres to [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/spec/v2.0.0.html).

## [2.4.0] - 2026-09-17

### Added
- **12-Question Diagnostic Engine**: Multi-step interactive assessment evaluating intake, qualifying, docs, packaging, routing, pipeline control, and tech budget.
- **Deterministic Recommendation Engine**: Algorithmic classification into 4 Operating Models (*Relationship-Led*, *Systemized*, *AI-Augmented*, *Capital Operator*).
- **8-Stage Architecture Map**: Visual interactive topology displaying System, AI, Human, and Capital Partner operational boundaries.
- **Automation Leak Audit**: Specific identification of manual work that can be transitioned to deterministic rules and AI synthesis.
- **Relationship Equity Protection Module**: Tailored analysis for referral and advisor business models to prevent client leak.
- **Print / PDF Generator**: Clean executive styling triggered via `window.print()` for instant presentation to partners or stakeholders.
- **Lead Capture & Dispatch**: Lightweight capture module configured with fallback hooks for direct CRM dispatch.

### Changed
- Refactored color tokens to an industrial dark fintech aesthetic (`#07090d` background, emerald/cyan accents).
- Standardized typography pairing: Plus Jakarta Sans for display and JetBrains Mono for system metrics.
