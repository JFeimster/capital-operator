# Project Governance: Capital Operator

## Overview
Capital Operator is maintained by Moonshine Capital's architecture and product development teams. This document outlines project decision-making, contributions, and architectural evolution.

## Roles
- **Maintainers**: Core engineering team with commit rights, responsible for release coordination and code reviews.
- **Contributors**: Ecosystem partners, advisors, and engineers who submit pull requests, documentation improvements, and tool updates.
- **Architectural Council**: Sets standards for diagnostic scoring weights, compliance boundaries, and capital partner integrations.

## Decision Making
Major changes to diagnostic scoring formulas, stages, or regulatory disclaimers require approval from at least two core maintainers.
