# The 4 Operating Models

## 1. Relationship-Led (0–18 pts)
- **Status Quo**: Deals tracked in email and memory. Document requests sent as plain text email attachments.
- **Throughput Limit**: 3–7 concurrent deals per person.
- **Primary Bottleneck**: Manual administrative drag; deals fall through cracks during vacation or volume spikes.

## 2. Systemized (19–32 pts)
- **Status Quo**: CRM pipeline with defined deal stages. Shared cloud folders with document upload links.
- **Throughput Limit**: 8–20 concurrent deals per person.
- **Primary Bottleneck**: Manual spreading of bank statements and handwriting credit memos consumes hours per deal.

## 3. AI-Augmented (33–44 pts)
- **Status Quo**: OCR document extraction live. AI-generated credit memorandums produced in 2 minutes. Human checkpoints govern all approvals.
- **Throughput Limit**: 25–60 concurrent deals per person.
- **Primary Bottleneck**: Lender follow-ups, condition clearing, and stipulation chasing still require manual coordination.

## 4. Capital Operator (45+ pts)
- **Status Quo**: Automated intake, zero-touch verification, dynamic multi-lender routing, and autonomous post-funding refinance monitoring.
- **Throughput Limit**: 75+ concurrent deals per person.
- **Primary Bottleneck**: Scaling distribution channels and managing multi-state regulatory compliance.
