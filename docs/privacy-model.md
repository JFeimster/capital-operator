# Privacy Model & Data Sovereignty

## Privacy by Design
1. **Zero Mandatory Account Creation**: Users can complete the full 12-question diagnostic, review their 8-stage architecture, and print/export their PDF roadmap without submitting an email address or creating an account.
2. **Local Evaluation**: Diagnostic scoring runs inside the browser client. Responses are not transmitted to backend servers during question answering.
3. **No Financial Credentials**: The diagnostic assesses operational workflow capabilities; it never requests bank account logins, SSNs, or proprietary underwriting secrets.
