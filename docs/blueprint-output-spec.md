# Blueprint Output Specification

## View Hierarchy
The Blueprint results screen is structured into 6 sequential sections:
1. **Operating Model Classification Banner**: Displays the detected tier, next capability leap, and estimated timeline to stabilize.
2. **Top Finding & Highest-Leverage Move**: High-contrast card detailing the single most impactful operational improvement.
3. **8-Stage Architecture & Tooling Matrix**: Complete stage-by-stage audit showing current friction, primary tool, system vs. human roles, and priority badge.
4. **Operational Efficiency Audit (Value Leaks)**: Table of acute manual friction points with business impact and urgency ratings.
5. **30-Day Implementation Roadmap**: Tabbed or sequential milestones across First 7 Days, Next 30 Days, and Later.
6. **Action Bar & Partner Dispatch**: Floating/docked controls to print PDF, copy markdown, or submit contact info to lock in the blueprint.
