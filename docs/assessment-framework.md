# Assessment Framework Specification

## The 12-Dimension Diagnostic Matrix
1. **Current Operating Posture**: Determines baseline operational role (Advisor, Operator, Platform, Referrer).
2. **Monthly Opportunity Volume**: Calibrates system throughput and automation requirements.
3. **Channel Diversity**: Identifies acquisition origins and partner attribution vulnerabilities.
4. **Pipeline Storage**: Audits central source of truth (email, spreadsheets, CRM, LOS).
5. **Pre-Qualification Logic**: Distinguishes gut-feel triage from structured credit box filters.
6. **Document Collection & Parsing**: Evaluates email attachment drag vs. OCR extraction.
7. **Lender Placement Strategy**: Identifies single-broker dependency vs. matrix matching.
8. **Follow-Up Automation**: Measures administrative chasing time.
9. **Acute Friction Points**: User-selected operational bottlenecks.
10. **Immediate Priorities**: 30-day focus areas.
11. **Software Budget**: Ensures tooling recommendations match economic reality.
12. **Desired Operational Involvement**: Calibrates hands-off partner vs. full deal desk recommendations.
