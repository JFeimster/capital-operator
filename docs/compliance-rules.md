# Compliance & Regulatory Rules

## Mandatory Guardrails
1. **Commercial Purpose Limitation**: Capital Operator is strictly intended for commercial enterprise debt and small business financing. No consumer lending (e.g. personal credit cards, auto loans, mortgages) is evaluated or offered.
2. **Not a Direct Commitment to Lend**: All generated blueprints, scores, and recommendations must explicitly state that they do not constitute a credit decision, term sheet, loan approval, or commitment to lend.
3. **Prohibited Marketing Copy**:
   - Never claim "guaranteed approval" or "100% funding rate".
   - Never use "instant cash" or "no credit check" language.
   - Never guarantee specific interest rates or terms without lender underwriting.
4. **Human Review Mandate**: AI credit memos and automated matches must always pass through human checkpoint validation prior to institutional lender submission.
5. **Affiliate Transparency**: Disclose that Moonshine Capital may receive affiliate compensation or partnership benefits from curated software tools and lending partners.
