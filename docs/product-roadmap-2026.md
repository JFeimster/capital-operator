# Product Roadmap 2026

## 2026 Strategic Milestones

### Q1-Q2 2026: Foundation & Distribution
- 12-question diagnostic matrix and deterministic scoring/recommendation engine — **LIVE**.
- 8-stage capital operating framework and transformation blueprints — **LIVE**.

- Production MCP endpoint at `/api/mcp` with capability discovery and externally smoke-tested JSON-RPC tools — **BETA transport**.
- Deterministic DSCR — **LIVE**.
- Capital-stack planning — **BETA**.
- Capital route matching — **SANDBOX**, informational only; not lender eligibility, pricing, approval, or live lender availability.
- Five Capital Operator Skills and four bounded operator-assistance agents — **BETA**, with consequential capital decisions human-controlled.
- Provider-agnostic document classification/parsing/synthesis contracts and deterministic extraction normalization — **SPECIFIED foundation**.
- Heron, Ocrolus, Validis, and other paid document/data adapters — **SPECIFIED** until explicitly configured.
- Contextual ecosystem capability matching, product handoffs, fallbacks, UI, and analytics — **BETA**.

### Q4 2026+: Distribution + Capital Clearing Infrastructure
- Public engineering-as-marketing tools and SEO/AEO entity layer — **PLANNED / Phase 4**.
- Authenticated workspace/tenant/deal/submission/offer/condition infrastructure — **PLANNED / Phase 5**.
- Accounting, CRM, financial-data, document-intelligence, and partner connectors are added only when justified and configured.
- Human approval gates remain mandatory for consequential capital decisions, exceptions, negotiations, and external representations.
