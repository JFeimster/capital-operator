# The 8 Workflow Stages of Modern Capital Operations

## Stage 1: Demand & Intake [CAPTURE]
- **Job**: Capture borrower inquiry cleanly across website, referral partner, or organic channels.
- **Key Capability**: Structured dynamic web intake capturing revenue, time in business, and credit score.

## Stage 2: Qualify & Triage [TRIAGE]
- **Job**: Instantly separate viable opportunities from dead-ends before human calendar time is booked.
- **Key Capability**: Rule-based credit box scoring and automated rejection of non-starters.

## Stage 3: Collect & Verify [INTAKE]
- **Job**: Gather complete financial documentation (bank statements, taxes, P&L) without manual email chasing.
- **Key Capability**: Secure borrower upload portal with automated reminder loops and fraud verification.

## Stage 4: Synthesize & Credit Memo [ANALYSIS]
- **Job**: Transform raw financial statements into an institutional-grade executive summary.
- **Key Capability**: Automated bank statement OCR and AI credit memo drafting.

## Stage 5: Capital Matching & Routing [MATCHING]
- **Job**: Match the deal to the optimal funding product (line of credit, term loan, SBA, asset-backed).
- **Key Capability**: Lender criteria matrix mapping borrower attributes directly to active credit boxes.

## Stage 6: Submission Management [SYNDICATION]
- **Job**: Coordinate parallel lender submissions without creating channel conflict or multiple hard credit pulls.
- **Key Capability**: Programmatic submission portals and status tracking.

## Stage 7: Clear & Close [FUNDING]
- **Job**: Chase underwriting conditions and verify closing wire confirmations.
- **Key Capability**: Automated stipulation checklists and closing coordination.

## Stage 8: Service & Compounding Lifecycle [RETENTION]
- **Job**: Monitor repayment progress and trigger timely refinancing or expansion credit before competitors do.
- **Key Capability**: Automated calendar triggers at 50% payoff milestones.
