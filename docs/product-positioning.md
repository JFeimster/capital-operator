# Product Positioning: Capital Operator

## Core Identity
**Capital Operator** is the diagnostic and blueprint engine built by **Moonshine Capital** to transform commercial borrowing demand into a scalable operating system.

## The Problem
Commercial loan brokers, fractional CFOs, CPAs, and advisory firms constantly encounter client capital opportunities (lines of credit, term debt, equipment loans, expansion capital). However, 85% of them manage these requests haphazardly:
- Intake arrives via informal email threads or WhatsApp messages.
- Bank statement PDFs sit in messy local download folders.
- Financial analysis is done by hand-typing figures into Excel.
- Applications are shotgunned or handed to 1 or 2 casual broker contacts with zero status tracking.

## The Solution
Capital Operator audits the user's operational posture across 12 diagnostic dimensions and instantly outputs:
1. **Operating Model Classification**: (Relationship-Led, Systemized, AI-Augmented, Capital Operator).
2. **8-Stage Infrastructure Architecture**: Clear software tooling and capability assignments from Demand to Compounding Lifecycle.
3. **Operational Leak Analysis**: Specific manual tasks costing the firm time and deal equity.
4. **Actionable 30-Day Roadmap**: Clear milestones for the first 7 days, 30 days, and beyond.

## Target Personas
1. **Advisors / Fractional CFOs**: Trusted advisors who need a turnkey way to monetize deal flow without becoming full-time brokers.
2. **Active Commercial Operators**: Established loan brokerages seeking to replace manual spreadsheet friction with AI ingestion and automated lender matching.
3. **Platform / Ecosystem Partners**: Vertical SaaS and B2B platforms looking to embed commercial capital solutions into their core software.
