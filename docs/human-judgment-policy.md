# Human Judgment Policy

## Autonomous vs. Supervised Workflows
Capital Operator strictly delineates between operations that can run autonomously and those requiring mandatory human intervention.

## Mandatory Human Checkpoints
1. **Marginal Deal Viability (Stage 2)**:
   - Clear passes and instant declines are handled by rules.
   - Borderline deals (e.g. strong revenue but low credit score due to medical debt) require human commercial review.
2. **AI Credit Memorandum Sign-Off (Stage 4)**:
   - AI outputs the initial financial summary and cash-flow spread.
   - A qualified underwriting analyst must review anomalous line items before external distribution.
3. **Lender Submission Approval (Stage 6)**:
   - The routing matrix suggests top lender matches.
   - A human broker confirms the final target list to prevent duplicate submissions or relationship conflict.
4. **Offer Counseling & Structuring (Stage 7)**:
   - Final terms, prepayment penalties, covenants, and personal guarantees must be explained to the borrower by a human advisor.
