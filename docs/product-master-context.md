# Product Master Context: Capital Operator

## Executive Summary
**Capital Operator** (powered by Moonshine Capital) is a high-conviction capital infrastructure diagnostic and blueprint generation platform designed for commercial debt advisors, fractional CFOs, commercial loan brokers, deal desk operators, and embedded fintech platforms. 

The system transitions informal, chaotic commercial loan brokerage workflows into a deterministic 8-stage operating system.

---

## Core Product Missions
1. **Friction Quantification**: Accurately diagnose where commercial deal flow leaks time, commission, and approval rate across 8 operational stages.
2. **Deterministic Blueprint Generation**: Output actionable, executive-ready 30-day transformation roadmaps and technology architectures based on a 12-question diagnostic matrix.
3. **Syndication Standard Setting**: Enforce institutional credit memorandum standards, reducing loan decline rates from typical broker averages (40-60%) to under 12%.
4. **Ecosystem Routing**: Seamlessly route pre-qualified commercial borrower demand to appropriate credit facilities across Moonshine Capital's 50+ debt fund syndicate network.

## 8 Operating Stages Framework
- **Stage 1: Intake & Capture** — Unified omnichannel borrower pre-screening.
- **Stage 2: Triage & Qualification** — Deterministic DSCR, debt-yield, and cash flow health scoring.
- **Stage 3: Document Assembly & Data Room** — Normalized electronic transaction extraction and debt schedules.
- **Stage 4: Packaging & Credit Memo** — Executive 2-page Institutional Credit Memorandum synthesis.
- **Stage 5: Lender Routing & Buy-Box Matching** — Automated alignment with 50+ debt fund criteria.
- **Stage 6: Submission & Communication** — Single-link confidential data rooms with tracked access.
- **Stage 7: Closing, Compliance & Funding** — Automated closing checklists, wire verification, fee attribution.
- **Stage 8: Post-Close Relationship Equity** — Automated 30/60/90/150-day retention and renewal triggers.
