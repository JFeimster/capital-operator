# Content & Tone Style Guide

## Brand Voice
- **Tone**: High-conviction, disciplined, analytical, institutional.
- **Archetype**: Modern dark fintech command center.
- **Perspective**: Senior capital operator who has processed hundreds of millions in loan volume.

## Vocabulary & Key Terms
- Use **"Capital Operator"**, not "loan salesperson" or "lead generator".
- Use **"Operating System"** or **"Infrastructure"**, not "quick hack" or "secret trick".
- Use **"Friction Point"** or **"Value Leak"**, not "mistake" or "bad habit".
- Use **"Credit Memo"** and **"Lender Routing Matrix"**, not "pitch sheet" or "blast list".
- Use **"Human Judgment Checkpoint"**, highlighting that AI synthesizes while humans decide.

## Banned AI Tropes & Slop
- Banned verbs: "Supercharge", "Skyrocket", "Empower", "Revolutionize", "10x your deals".
- Banned visuals: Neon purple-to-blue gradient text, glassmorphism cards, glowing borders.
- Keep numbers objective and grounded in real commercial finance workflows.
