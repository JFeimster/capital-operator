# Marketing Site Copy & Positioning — Capital Operator

## Core Value Proposition
Turn Borrowing Demand Into An Operating System.

## Supporting Thesis
The commercial capital advisory industry loses up to 40% of actionable transaction yield to slow intake, messy bank statement spreadsheets, and blind lender shotgunning. Capital Operator provides the diagnostic tools and operating blueprints to automate clerical intake, standardise underwriting memos, and accelerate funding velocity.

## Audience Personas
1. **Advisors & Fractional CFOs**: Stop spending 15 hours per client reformatting balance sheets. Offer turnkey commercial credit packages without administrative bloat.
2. **Commercial Loan Brokers**: Close 3x more deals with automated bank statement OCR, instant DSCR calculations, and lender buy-box routing.
3. **B2B Platforms & Fintechs**: Programmatically ingest borrowing demand from existing merchant workflows and monetize with zero credit risk.
4. **Institutional Debt Providers**: Receive clean, pre-underwritten loan files that meet credit committee guidelines on first submission.
