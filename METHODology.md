# Capital Operator Diagnostic Methodology

## The 8-Stage Operating Architecture
Capital Operator breaks down commercial borrowing operations into 8 distinct workflow stages:

1. **Demand & Intake [CAPTURE]**: Programmatic lead capture replacing email threads.
2. **Qualify & Triage [TRIAGE]**: 60-second credit box qualification filtering non-starters.
3. **Collect & Verify [INTAKE]**: Secure document upload portals with automated reminders.
4. **Synthesize & Credit Memo [ANALYSIS]**: Financial OCR spreading and AI memo drafting.
5. **Capital Matching & Routing [MATCHING]**: Dynamic lender matrix matching deal attributes.
6. **Submission Management [SYNDICATION]**: Coordinated parallel submissions without credit shotgunning.
7. **Clear & Close [FUNDING]**: Real-time condition clearing and closing verification.
8. **Service & Compounding Lifecycle [RETENTION]**: Continuous renewal and refinance monitoring.

## The 4 Operating Tiers
- **Relationship-Led (0–18 pts)**: High human touch, informal memory, spreadsheet-driven.
- **Systemized (19–32 pts)**: CRM pipelines, cloud storage, manual credit analysis.
- **AI-Augmented (33–44 pts)**: OCR extraction, AI credit synthesis, human checkpoints.
- **Capital Operator (45+ pts)**: Programmatic routing, syndication rails, autonomous renewal loops.
