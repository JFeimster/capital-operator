# Capital Infrastructure for Commercial Loan Brokers

Transform an email-driven desk into an automated deal syndication engine.

## The Operator's Challenge
Most commercial brokers max out their capacity around 10-15 active deals per month. The bottleneck isn't origination; it's administrative friction:
- Chasing missing bank statements
- Manually calculating monthly cash flows
- Sending files to lenders who take two weeks to decline

## How Infrastructure Unlocks Scale
1. **Dynamic Borrower Portals**: Borrowers complete structured checklists before an underwriter touches the file.
2. **Instant Credit Box Verification**: Know within 60 seconds which lenders have appetite.
3. **Structured Packaging**: Clean, pre-packaged credit memos that jump to the front of lender review queues.
