# The Modern Commercial Capital Stack

Navigating commercial financing requires understanding where each debt instrument fits into a borrower's capital structure.

## Capital Layers
1. **Senior Bank & SBA Debt (Low Cost, High Friction)**: Prime rate + 1-3%. Requires strong DSCR, collateral, and comprehensive financial history.
2. **Asset-Backed Lending (Collateral Driven)**: Secured by accounts receivable, inventory, or machinery. Flexible liquidity without rigid covenants.
3. **Revenue-Based Financing & Working Capital (Velocity Driven)**: Fast underwriting based on cash flow consistency and gross receipts.
4. **Mezzanine & Subordinated Credit (Growth / Leverage)**: Junior to senior lenders, higher cost, ideal for acquisitions and recapitalizations.
