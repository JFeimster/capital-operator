# Diagnostic Engine & Scoring Methodology

The Capital Operator diagnostic engine evaluates an organization's workflow across 12 targeted assessment vectors.

## Scoring Dimensions
1. **Intake Friction**: Measurement of borrower qualification accuracy and abandonment rates.
2. **Underwriting Latency**: Time elapsed from first submission to completed credit evaluation.
3. **Document Velocity**: Automated versus manual handling of financial statements and tax filings.
4. **Lender Placement Precision**: Ratio of targeted lender approvals versus blind shotgunning.
5. **Relationship Equity**: Lifetime value capture via post-funding renewal triggers.
