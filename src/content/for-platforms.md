# Embedded Capital Infrastructure for B2B Platforms & Fintechs

Monetize transaction flow with institutional commercial lending rails.

## Embedded Lending Architecture
Vertical SaaS and B2B platforms maintain proprietary insights into customer cash flows, invoicing, and sales. Capital Operator provides the operational and technological blueprints to connect that data with institutional debt providers.

- **Programmatic Webhook Ingestion**: Route capital requests directly into automated underwriting workflows.
- **Zero Credit Risk**: Place loans through vetted partner networks without balance sheet exposure.
- **Seamless White-Label Flow**: Maintain native brand trust throughout the borrower journey.
