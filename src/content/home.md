# Capital Operator: Institutional Infrastructure for Commercial Debt

Turn fragmented commercial borrowing demand into a predictable, scalable operating system.

## The Operator Thesis
Most loan brokerages and financial advisory firms operate like artisan workshops rather than manufacturing lines. Individual advisors juggle scattered inbox threads, unvetted borrower calls, and manual spreadsheet entries.

When operational infrastructure is missing:
- **Underwriting Turnaround Is Sluggish**: 18+ days from initial inquiry to lender submission.
- **Conversion Yield Collapses**: Up to 40% of viable loan requests drop out due to documentation fatigue.
- **Lender Trust Erodes**: Incomplete or mismatched submissions get deprioritized by tier-1 credit committees.

Capital Operator audits your entire pipeline across 8 standardized operating stages, highlights high-cost automation leaks, and provides a clear 30-day implementation blueprint.
