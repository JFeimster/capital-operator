# Capital Infrastructure for Fractional CFOs & Debt Advisors

Institutionalize commercial debt placement without turning your consulting practice into a chaotic broker desk.

## The Advisory Dilemma
Fractional CFOs and financial advisors have high-trust relationships with CEOs and business owners. When those clients need growth capital, advisors often get dragged into manual document collation and random lender inquiries.

## The Solution: Operating Infrastructure
- **Standardized Client Intake**: Branded intake links allow clients to enter financials directly.
- **Automated Debt Modeling**: Calculate true borrowing capacity and DSCR in minutes.
- **Curated Syndication Routing**: Connect directly to institutional lenders matched to your client's industry and risk profile.
