# The 8-Stage Commercial Debt Operating Architecture

Capital Operator structures the commercial lending lifecycle into eight deterministic stages:

### 1. Multi-Channel Intake & Triage
Dynamic digital questionnaires screen borrowers at first touch. Captures DSCR, revenue thresholds, credit bands, and time-in-business upfront.

### 2. Deterministic Pre-Qualification
Rule engines immediately route or decline submissions based on hard parameters, preventing hours wasted on non-fundable scenarios.

### 3. Automated Document Collection & Scrubbing
Secure borrower portal with OCR extraction for 6 months bank PDFs, profit & loss statements, balance sheets, and tax returns.

### 4. Financial Spreading & Credit Memorandum
Automated data parsing generates a normalized debt-service coverage model and executive 2-page credit memo for lender review.

### 5. Algorithmic Lender Matching
Deal profile mapped against 50+ institutional credit guidelines to target lenders with active appetite for the asset class.

### 6. Governed Parallel Submissions
Offers solicited systematically without shotgunning borrower credit or creating lender channel conflict.

### 7. Closing & Funding Coordination
Clear stipulation trackers, payoff letters, and escrow logistics coordinate closing in days rather than weeks.

### 8. Post-Funding Relationship Equity
Automated milestone monitoring (60, 120, 240 days) to service renewal opportunities, credit line extensions, and partner attribution.
