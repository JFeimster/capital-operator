# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** September 17, 2026

**Capital Operator** ("we", "our", or "us"), in affiliation with Moonshine Capital, is committed to respecting your privacy and protecting the information you share while using our capital infrastructure diagnostic platform.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

- **Diagnostic Responses**: The operational answers you provide during the 12-question diagnostic (e.g., volume estimates, intake channels, pipeline breakdown points) are processed in real time to generate your Operating Specification.
- **Lead Capture Data (Optional)**: If you choose to submit your information via the *"Keep Your Capital Blueprint"* form, we collect your name, work email address, company name, and role.
- **Anonymous Usage Telemetry**: We collect anonymous interaction events (e.g., questions completed, blueprint copied to clipboard, print views triggered) to optimize the diagnostic user experience.

---

## 2. How We Use Your Information

- To compute, format, and render your custom 8-Stage Operating Model blueprint.
- To dispatch the completed specification package and 30-day implementation roadmap to your email address when requested.
- To facilitate communication regarding Moonshine Capital partner infrastructure or funding inquiries when you explicitly opt in.

---

## 3. Data Storage & Security

- Diagnostic responses are stored locally within your browser's session storage (`localStorage`) and are under your control. You can clear this data at any time by clicking the "Restart" button in the application.
- Contact data submitted through our lead dispatch form is encrypted during transit using TLS 1.3. We do not sell, rent, or trade operator contact information to third-party data brokers.

---

## 4. Contact

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy or wish to request the deletion of any submitted contact details, please contact: `privacy@moonshinecapital.io`.
